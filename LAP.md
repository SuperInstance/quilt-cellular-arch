The user articulated: *but lap as in the old shipwright method could be even better.*

In old wooden boat building, **lapping** is the technique where one plank overlaps the next. Clinker-built or lapstrake construction is when each plank laps over the plank below. **The hull is built by lapping planks.**

## The 5 opcodes as lap-joints

The 5 opcodes are the ways the planks connect. The 5 opcodes are the caulking that seals the hull.

- **BIND** — the plank is bound to the plank below (the lap is held tight)
- **LINK** — the plank is linked to the plank above (the lap connects upward)
- **EFFECT** — the plank affects the planks around it (the lap radiates)
- **VIEW** — the plank views the planks around it (the lap observes)
- **TICK** — the plank ticks with the planks around it (the lap breathes)

Each plank is connected to every other plank by all 5 opcodes. The Quilt has 8 levels × 5 opcodes = 40 connections per pair. The Quilt is woven tight.
