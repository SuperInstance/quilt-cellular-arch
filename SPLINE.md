# SPLINE.md — The Spline, The Function, The Captain's Quilt of Understanding

The user articulated the deepest insight of the framework: *the spline of what boats I might see as a step forward are shaped by the captain and son in 1974 who replaced the old Atlas engine with what at the time was a modern 6-71N latest edition. But now is seen as the old but stable horse.*

The spline is the 8th level of the operation. The spline is the trajectory of the captain's understanding. The spline is the gravity that pulls the future toward the past.

## The function is the captain

The function (wanting to chop wood, wanting to catch salmon) is the invariant. The function is the captain. The function selects the next implement.

The grandpa's function: *chop wood to heat the house*. This function selected each of 3 houses (each with a wood stove and wood source nearby). This function selected each of 3 axe handles + 2 axe heads. The function persisted through all changes.

Casey's function: *catch salmon and other fish from the sea*. This function selected 4 boats. This function will select the next boat. The function persists through all changes.

## The option-set is the product of means and quilt of understanding

The captain's option-set is the set of possible implements the function could select. The option-set is the **product of means and quilt of understanding**.

- **Means** = financial/social capital (the captain's wallet, the captain's network)
- **Quilt of understanding** = epistemic capital (the captain's accumulated experience, the captain's knowledge of what a good implement is)

The bigger the means, the more boats you can choose from. The bigger the quilt, the more boats you can *see* as possible.

Casey's option-set:
- 2000: means=1, quilt=1, option_set=0
- 2010: means=3, quilt=2, option_set=3
- 2015: means=5, quilt=3, option_set=6
- 2020: means=7, quilt=4, option_set=11

The option-set grew as the means and the quilt grew.

## The spline is the trajectory of past choices

The spline is a smooth curve that passes through every past choice. The spline is the captain's perception of "step forward." The spline is the gravity that pulls future choices toward the past.

Casey's spline:
- 2000: quality 0.3 (Casey's 1st boat)
- 2010: quality 0.5 (Casey's 2nd boat)
- 2015: quality 0.6 (Casey's 3rd boat)
- 2020: quality 0.55 (Eileen, the 4th boat — the "old but stable horse")

The spline passes through these 4 points. The spline shapes what Casey sees as a step forward. Casey would see a step forward as a boat of quality 0.55-0.7. Casey would NOT see a step forward as a boat of quality 0.9 (too far from the spline).

The 1974 captain's 6-71N Detroit is a *point on the spline* (the boat's engine). At the time, the 6-71N was modern. Now, it's the old but stable horse. **The 1974 choice is a point on the spline. The current captain's perception passes through that point.**

## The implement shapes the understanding

The implement shapes the captain's understanding. The 4th captain (the shipwright) replaced most of Eileen's planks. The 4th captain's logging operation produced the wood. The 4th captain's shipwright work shaped the 5th captain's understanding of what a fishing boat is.

The 1974 captain's choice of 6-71N shaped the 5th captain's understanding of what an engine is. The 5th captain sees the 6-71N as the old but stable horse, not as a relic.

**The implement shapes the understanding. The understanding shapes the spline. The spline shapes the next implement. The cycle continues.**

The 8 levels of the operation:

The first 6 are implements. The 7th and 8th are invariants. The 7th is the function. The 8th is the spline. **The 7th and 8th are both the captain. The 7th is the captain's function. The 8th is the captain's history.**
