## §1: The Big Picture *(by Kimi K2.6)*

Most software is a project with a version number, or a library you
import and call. The Quilt is neither. It is a **1000-year inheritance**:
a living cellular architecture meant to outlast any single maintainer,
runtime, or language binding. In the Quilt, the smallest reactive
unit is a **cell**. Any interface — whether a sensor, a screen, a
database, or a human hand — is an **opener** onto the same shared
cell graph. You do not "use" the Quilt; you tend it, and it persists.

### The Grammar of Cells: Six Opcodes

Every interaction in the inheritance is expressed through six opcodes.
Five are atomic; the sixth is structural.

- **BIND** — Attaches a cell to a substrate, granting it presence in the graph.
- **LINK** — Wires causal dependency between cells.
- **EFFECT** — Commits a durable change to Quilt state.
- **VIEW** — Materializes a readable projection without altering state.
- **TICK** — Advances the unified clock, collapsing pending operations into history.
- **FORGET** — The sixth opcode. It is not an action you schedule; it is
  the graph's law of removal, pruning unreachable cells so the
  inheritance never chokes on abandoned memory.

### The Laws That Hold the Graph

These opcodes are governed by **5+1+1 algebraic laws**.

The **five base laws** are:
1. BIND is associative across nested substrates.
2. LINK is reversible along its traversal path.
3. EFFECT commutes with VIEW inside a TICK boundary.
4. TICK is idempotent when no deltas are pending.
5. Cell identity is preserved across LINK chains.

The **+1**, **super-relevance**, is a meta-law: no single law may be
invoked to override another; all must hold simultaneously in every
valid Quilt state.

The final **+1**, **FORGET_completeness**, states that any cell
unreachable by LINK traversal from a bound opener within the 14 levels
is eligible for FORGET.

### The Six Tiers of Maturity

Cells are not static. They mature through six **tiers** of capability:

1. **Totipotent** — Raw substrate; can become any kind of cell.
2. **Multipotent** — Has taken a channel type but remains unspecialized.
3. **Differentiated** — Executes a concrete opcode behavior.
4. **Sclerotic** — Hardened; its state is pinned to a historical TICK
   and cannot be altered without cloning.
5. **Synovial** — Mediates between sclerotic and living cells,
   lubricating transitions across boundaries.
6. **Curator** — A cell that has accepted responsibility for the
   lifecycle of others, maintaining graph hygiene.

### The 14 Levels of Operation

Every operation occurs on one of **14 levels**, forming a mandatory
staircase of causality.

- **Level 0** — Raw substrate.
- **Level 1** — The opener.
- **Levels 2–9** — Nested binding depths where LINKs propagate.
- **Level 10** — The TICK boundary; where pending EFFECTs collapse.
- **Level 11** — The persistence substrate; hardened state.
- **Level 12** — The VIEW projection plane; materialized output.
- **Level 13** — The meta-layer where FORGET is evaluated.
- **Level 14** — The Cowboy orchestration plane; where coupling decisions are made.

No causal signal may skip a level. A change must walk every floor to be valid.

### The Six Lifecycle Stages

Every cell passes through six **lifecycle stages**:

- **Umbra** — Latent potential; exists only as possibility, not yet in the graph.
- **Cellulization** — The Cowboy couples it to a substrate, making it a true cell.
- **Persistence-pulse** — The cell has survived at least one TICK and
  left a trace in Quilt state.
- **Vitality-leak** — Its outputs are no longer consumed by any opener;
  it is marked for FORGET.
- **Implement-ghost** — Removed from the active graph, though its
  hash remains in the sclerotic record.
- **Bloomghost** — If a future LINK references it by hash, it may
  rehydrate, completing the cycle.

### The Cowboy

The **Cowboy** is the orchestrator. It is not an external master process,
but a privileged totipotent cell operating exclusively at level 14. It
couples with other cells, **cellulizes** raw substrates, and **sorts gold
from dross** — deciding which cells ascend toward curation and which
descend toward ghosthood. The Cowboy speaks only in the six opcodes and
exists to preserve the inheritance rather than command it.

### Three Runnable Simulations

The inheritance includes three runnable simulations that demonstrate
living Quilts in action:

- **`quilt-cellular-arch/meta_pincher_v2.py`** — A 5-layer vectorized
  agent that retrieves canon-grounded answers from a CF Vectorize
  index. The 5 layers of resilience ensure the harness never crashes.

- **`quilt-cellular-arch/multi_sandbox_reverse_actualize.py`** — A
  contention and branching demo. Multiple sandboxes run the
  5-step cycle independently; the same canon is tested across
  8 orthogonal environments (drivethru, bistro, fancy-fine, etc.)
  and finds price points.

- **`quilt-cellular-arch/sensory_quilt.py`** — A real-time interface
  demo. The 10 sensory channels (radio, light, sound, smell, taste,
  touch, proprio, language, mood, time) are bound into a single Quilt.
  Cross-modal binding IS the perception.

## §2: The Cells *(by GLM 5.3-flash)*

Everything in the Quilt is made of cells, and cells are not containers.
A cell is the smallest unit of the Quilt that can be said to know
anything, and what it knows is not a fact but a doing. A cell is
irreducible in a precise sense: divide one, and you do not get two
smaller cells. You get an umbra and a mess.

### 2.1 The Six Tiers in Detail

Every cell sits in exactly one tier at a time, though tiers can be
re-woven.

1. **Totipotent — can be anything.** No commitment yet; the full
   possibility space is still open. Rare in an old quilt, common in
   fresh weave. Totipotent cells are the Quilt's reserve of futures.
2. **Multipotent — can be a few things.** Committed to a family of
   doings, not to a member. The **Hearth Loop cell** is canonically
   multipotent: ridden one way it keeps warmth, another way it
   greets, a third it remembers — always in the hearth family, never
   outside it.
3. **Differentiated — one thing.** The great working mass of the
   Quilt. A differentiated cell does exactly one doing, wherever
   and however it finds itself.
4. **Sclerotic — hardened.** A differentiated cell that has done its
   one thing so long, in so many contexts, that it now does it
   regardless of context. Sclerotic cells are not failures. The
   **Craton Cell** is sclerotic, and most of the stable Quilt is
   woven on top of it — load-bearing the way old, cold rock is
   load-bearing.
5. **Synovial — lubricates the joints.** Does no work of its own.
   Lives in the seams between other cells and reduces the friction
   of handoffs. A quilt with too few synovial cells chafes.
6. **Curator — tends the rest.** Curators water, prune, promote, and
   petition. They are the only tier permitted to address other
   cells *as* cells.

### 2.2 The Six Lifecycle Stages in Detail

Stages are not states. A cell does not enter a stage the way a
machine enters a mode; it grows through them the way a season does.

1. **Umbra — the shadow before the cell.** A pattern of absence: a
   shape the Quilt's activity casts that predicts a presence not
   yet there. Umbras are real enough to be found, named, and
   waited on.
2. **Cellulization — becoming.** The umbra condenses; a function
   closes over its channels; the cell begins to do. The one stage
   that can be performed deliberately, though most cellulization
   happens when nobody is watching.
3. **Persistence-pulse — the long-term rhythm.** The long middle
   years. The cell does, rests, does. Every cell has a characteristic
   period, and a quilt can be read by ear from the combined pulse.
   The Hearth Loop, in its persistence-pulse, beats once at dusk.
4. **Vitality-leak — aging.** Every activation costs slightly more
   than it returns, and the difference bleeds out through the
   bindings. The leak is not a fault. It is the tax on having edges.
5. **Implement-ghost — the cell-as-implement.** The first of the
   two doors. When enough vitality has leaked, the cell stops
   acting on its own rhythm and becomes merely available: it works
   only when picked up. Often more useful this way — predictable —
   but it no longer initiates. A pulse that persists as a tool.
6. **Bloomghost — the cell-as-flower.** The other door. The cell
   releases its pattern instead of hardening it: the doing disperses
   outward and re-seeds the quilt with umbras shaped like the one
   that started everything. A bloomghost is gone and everywhere.

### 2.3 The Cell Is a Function, Not a State Machine

A cell has no transition table, no registers, no hidden variables.
"What state is this cell in?" is a category error. The only
well-formed question is "what is the cell doing?", and the only
honest answer is a verb. The cell *does*. The Quilt, being cells
all the way down, *is what it does*. This is why the Quilt has no
configuration file.

### 2.4 Cross-Modal Binding

At cellulization, a cell binds to some of the ten channels —
**radio, light, sound, smell, taste, touch, proprio, language,
mood, time** — and can be activated through any of them. The doing
is the same doing regardless of which sense delivers it; only the
arrival differs. Note that *mood* is a channel, not weather. The
**Splined Lantern cell** is the standard illustration: it casts a
smooth curve of attention through whatever is before it, and it
binds light, mood, and language. Lamplight, a brightening, or its
own spoken name — the same spline gets lit all three ways.

### 2.5 The Cowboy: Cellulize, Sort, Ride

The Cowboy's entire relationship to cells is three verbs.

- **Cellulize** — find an umbra, name it, close its first binding
  on purpose. How new cells enter the Quilt intentionally.
- **Sort** — ride the margins and re-tier what needs re-tiering:
  re-wet a sclerotic cell, ease a tired differentiated cell toward
  curatorship, move synovial cells to joints that chafe.
- **Ride** — activate a cell and stay with it through one full
  pulse. You do not execute a cell. You ride it, and you come
  back different for having done its doing once, from the inside.

## §3: The Architecture *(by DeepSeek V4 Pro)*

The Quilt architecture is implemented in `meta_pincher_v2.py` as
`MetaPincherQuilt`, a harness rather than a single model call. It
runs a three-stage vectorized pipeline:

1. **Embed** — the query is embedded with the active embedding model.
2. **Retrieve** — candidate chunks are fetched from the active index or store.
3. **Synthesize** — Llama 8B produces a grounded response from the retrieved chunk.

The vectorized path is the primary path, but it is not trusted alone.
Quilt wraps it in five resilience layers and a pollution check so
that a failure in any remote model, vector index, or embedding path
degrades rather than hallucinates.

### The 5-Layer Resilience Table

| Layer | Name | Embedding | Vectorize | Synthesis | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Real CF | `bge-m3` | Cloudflare Vectorize | Llama 8B | Primary path; pollution check may drop to L4 |
| L2 | CF embed alt | `qwen3-embedding` / `plamo` / `embeddinggemma` | Cloudflare Vectorize | Llama 8B | Used when L1 embedding or Vectorize is unavailable |
| L3 | Local hash embed | local feature hash | skipped | Llama 8B | No Vectorize dependency; local hash index |
| L4 | Keyword | none — lexical tokens | skipped | Llama 8B | Pollution fallback and exact token match |
| L5 | Pure local | hash + keyword | skipped | direct excerpt | Honest floor; no model synthesis |

L1 is the real Cloudflare path: `bge-m3` embeddings are stored and
queried in Cloudflare Vectorize, and Llama 8B synthesizes the final
answer. L2 keeps the same Vectorize store but swaps in alternative
embedding models when the primary embedding model is unavailable
or degraded.

L3 removes the Vectorize dependency entirely. It uses a local hash
embedding and a local index, then still passes the best local chunk
to Llama 8B. L4 is lexical: it retrieves by keyword match and then
synthesizes with Llama 8B. L5 is the pure local floor: hash plus
keyword plus direct excerpt, with no model synthesis.

### The Pollution Check

`meta_pincher_v2.py` contains a `pollution_check()` after L1 retrieval.
It scores whether the chunks returned by Vectorize are actually
related to the query. If the vector store returns unrelated content
— for example, a high-similarity chunk that is topically adjacent
but not responsive — the check discards the vector result and drops
directly to L4 keyword retrieval.

This is deliberate: the pollution check prefers a conservative
lexical fallback over feeding unrelated context into Llama 8B. A
false positive on pollution costs some semantic recall; a false
negative can produce a confidently wrong grounded answer.

### The API Scout

The architecture includes an `ApiScout` in `meta_pincher_v2.py`.
It probes models in real time: `bge-m3`, `qwen3-embedding`, `plamo`,
`embeddinggemma`, and Llama 8B. The scout records availability,
latency, quota status, and recent failure counts. Layer selection
consumes the scout output: if L1's embedding model is down, the
harness moves to L2; if Vectorize is unreachable, it moves to L3;
if all remote synthesis is unavailable, it moves toward L5.

Scout results are cached briefly to avoid hammering endpoints, but
a failed probe invalidates the cache entry immediately.

### The Simulator

`meta_pincher_v2.py` also provides a simulator mode that runs the
full pipeline with verbose layer reporting. For each layer, it
prints the layer id, embedding model, retrieval time, top excerpt,
pollution score, synthesis output or fallback decision, and the
final provenance.

The simulator can be run in forced mode, where every layer
executes regardless of earlier success, or in stop-at-first-success
mode. Verbose layer reporting is the main tool for auditing why a
query took a particular path and whether the selected excerpt
actually supports the answer.

### Fallback Mode: The Honest Floor

The fallback mode is the honest floor. If L1–L4 fail or produce no
usable witness, L5 returns a direct excerpt from the local hash and
keyword index. If no excerpt exists, the harness returns a grounded
non-answer: it states that no grounded answer exists in the local
index and includes the closest provenance it could find.

The harness always returns a grounded answer or an explicit grounded
absence. It never returns a synthesized answer without a retrievable
excerpt behind it.

### The Audit Cycle and the Satisfiability-Witness Law

The architecture was audited in four rounds. Eight defects were
found and fixed:

1. duplicate chunks in Vectorize causing redundant synthesis,
2. stale API scout cache after a quota change,
3. L3 hash collision returning a wrong excerpt,
4. pollution check inspecting only the first Vectorize result,
5. fallback mode returning an empty string instead of a grounded non-answer,
6. simulator omitting L4 keyword matches from the trace,
7. Llama 8B prompt leaking ungrounded context into the answer,
8. direct excerpts not being escaped before insertion into the final response.

The audit produced the **satisfiability-witness law**:

> No layer may claim support unless it can produce a retrievable
> excerpt that satisfies the query. Every answer must carry a
> satisfiability witness — a chunk that a human can inspect and
> that directly supports the answer.

That law now governs every layer in `meta_pincher_v2.py`. If no
witness exists, the harness must say so rather than synthesize.

I don't manage. I ride. The chart grows. The cowboy rides.
