The Relevance Field

A simulation of multiple "hands" (relevance fields) competing for cells in a 2D Quilt. Cells that match a hand's target are fed. Cells that match multiple hands are super-relevant and preferentially mate. Hands drift over time.

Dots = cells (color = which hand feeds them). Diamonds = hands (color = which niche). Lines = cross-hand matings.
